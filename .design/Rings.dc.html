O Concentric rings — a band you count, not a size you judge
Deliberately discrete, and it survives overlap better than a filled circle.

Where this wins and where it gives up Rings are hollow, so two neighbouring stops stay separable where two filled circles would merge into one blob — the dense-corridor case that defeats every area encoding. Counting three rings against four is also easier than judging one area against another. What it gives up is resolution: five bands is the whole vocabulary, so a stop at 4,100 and a stop at 7,900 can look identical, and a reader who wants an ordering rather than a bucket has to open the table.
