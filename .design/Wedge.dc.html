Sweep — share of line
A different question, deliberately Every other draft here encodes how many riders a stop carries; this one encodes what fraction of its line it carries, which is the shareOfLine column the table already computes and the map has never shown. Because the shares of one line sum to a whole turn, a reader can see at a glance whether a line is one dominant station or a dozen even ones. The cost is that angle decodes worse than length or area, and that every wedge is the same overall size, so a busy bus line and a quiet one look alike until you read the legend.
