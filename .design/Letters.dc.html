JJ The letter, because the hues do not separate
Click a strategy. The colour distances come from the palette validator.
Metro’s six line colours are a brand, so the app cannot reorder or re-step them the way it could its own selection palette. Run through the validator they fail three checks, and the failures are between lines that meet at the same stations. A mark that shows several lines at one point therefore needs something that is not a hue, and a letter is the thing every rider already reads.
