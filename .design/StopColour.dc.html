What this has to argue with CONTEXT.md says the Stop Measure is carried on the map "by fill and stroke, never by a second colour ramp: colour already means which line." This draft does not remove that rule so much as invert it — identity moves to the stroke and magnitude takes the fill. It works while two or three lines are selected. At five it asks a reader to tell five thin rings apart at a four-pixel radius, which is the case the rule was written for.
