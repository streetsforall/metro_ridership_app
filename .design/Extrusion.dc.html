What tilting the map costs you A tilted view puts near bins in front of far ones at a larger apparent size, so two equal figures stop looking equal and a tall bin hides whatever stands behind it. Pitch and bearing also become part of what a reader sees, which means the URL has to carry them or a shared link opens a different picture. Set against that, fill-extrusion is a real MapLibre layer, so this is the one length encoding that needs no sprite sheet and no canvas overlay.
