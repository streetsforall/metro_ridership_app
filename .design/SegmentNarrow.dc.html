What this still needs that the app does not have A running sum needs the stops in route order, and a stop’s identity is deliberately its normalised name rather than Metro’s row order (ADR-0013). Recovering the order means joining stop_times.txt, which the roadmap already names as how direction could come back. Direction is collapsed, so one width per segment is both directions added together rather than a train’s load. The narrower ramp helps here too: a thin line invites a reader to trace a route, where a thick ribbon invites them to read a volume the data cannot support. The profile should close at zero and does not. Stop sums miss line totals by a median 0.06% and up to 5.10%, one-sided upward, so the 640 riders still aboard at Long Beach are the accumulated error. Drawing that endpoint rather than trimming it is what makes this chart a data check as well as a picture.
