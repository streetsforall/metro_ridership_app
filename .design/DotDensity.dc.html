The objection that also sank the smoothed density surface A dot has to be drawn somewhere, and every position other than the stop itself puts riders in a place that never reported them — the same complaint that made the density layer hex-binned rather than smoothed. Scattering also breaks the gap rule twice over: an empty patch reads as "nobody" when it may mean "no report", which is why a stop with no data still draws its dashed ring here. What it buys in exchange is that quantity becomes countable, and the marks stay small enough that a busy corridor reads as texture rather than as one merged circle.
