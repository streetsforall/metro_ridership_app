One more worth adding, which you did not name Service frequency comes straight out of the stop_times.txt the ingest already downloads: trips per hour at each stop, by day of week, on the same grain the ridership sits on. It is the cheapest layer on this page by a wide margin and it is the one that changes what a low number means, because a stop with few boardings and four buses an hour is a different argument from a stop with few boardings and one.
The constraint every row inherits Each of these is shareable state and has to survive a URL round-trip through both halves of useUserDashboardInput, which draft Y draws. The harder limit is the reader rather than the link: the stop circles are the subject, and every layer beneath them competes for the same paint. One background layer at a time is the honest cap, and a picker that enforces it is a radio rather than a rack of checkboxes.
