Three seams assembling creates, and where F now stands beside it The measure control changes meaning. In F it chooses what the map draws; here the map draws both figures always, so the control only orders the list. Its label has to say so, or a reader will keep expecting the map to respond. The label layer needs a rank, and the obvious rank is the list’s. MapLibre keeps whichever labels win the collision, sorted by symbol-sort-key. Point that key at whatever the list is ranked by and the two views agree about which stops matter; point it anywhere else and they quietly disagree. F is a live direction again, and R does not replace it. F and five variations on its hover card have their own page now. R is still the assembly of F, L and N, so the two overlap and only one can ship — and the first line above is the choice between them, not a note about a retired draft. Two bars is twice the ink, and this map has forty-seven stops rather than eight hundred. The A Line’s southern run already shows bars touching. Bus at full scale needs either a zoom floor below which the spikes collapse back to circles, or the gated fetch stays gated.
