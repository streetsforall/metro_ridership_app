Four lines call at 7th Street / Metro Center, so buildStopView emits four features at one coordinate and the circle layer paints them in line order at 75% opacity. The station does not draw as four marks. It draws as one bullseye whose every band is a blend.

Across the whole rail payload 110 rail stops report ridership. 96 are served by one line, 12 by two, Union Station by three and 7th Street by four, so 14 stops — one in eight — draw more circles than a reader can count.
The fill stops meaning which line exactly where the reader most needs it to This project reserves colour for line identity everywhere on the map, and at these fourteen stops the reserved channel gives an answer that is false: the core of the 7th Street mark is a dusty pink that belongs to no Metro line, and the two intermediate bands are 0.4 px and 2.2 px wide. The panel meanwhile lists four rows for this station, so the two views of one selection disagree about how many things are here. Every redesign on this page starts from that disagreement rather than from a preference about shape.
