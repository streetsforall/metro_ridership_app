What it buys, and what it costs People read length far more accurately than area, so a spike map answers "how much busier" where circles only answer "busier". Hue stays free to mean which line. The cost is upward occlusion — a tall spike crosses whatever sits north of it, which is why 7th Street reaches into Historic Broadway here — and MapLibre draws no bars natively, so this is a symbol layer with a generated sprite per height or a canvas overlay.
