Hover is the step this design cannot have on a touch screen Two of the three steps need a pointer. A phone has no hover, so the peek has no trigger and the first tap has to do the work of both. The design that survives the translation is tap for the open card and a second tap on the card to pin it, which means the peek is a desktop refinement rather than the foundation — and a draft that leans on it is a draft that will need a second design later. A card that changes size under the cursor moves the thing being pointed at. The card grows downward and leftward from a fixed top-right corner here, so the circle a reader is hovering never ends up underneath it. Growing from the cursor instead would put the chart where the stop was.
