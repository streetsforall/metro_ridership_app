Why this one argues with nothing Hue keeps meaning which line, exactly as CONTEXT.md requires, because the measure moves onto a channel the dashboard has never used. Texture also survives what colour does not — greyscale printing, forced-colours mode, and a reader who cannot separate the ramp's middle steps. The cost is resolution: five hatch steps is about as many as a fifteen-pixel circle can hold, so this reads bands rather than values, and a dense bus corridor at low zoom fills with rules until the circles merge.
