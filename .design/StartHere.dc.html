READ THE FIGURES WITH CARE Some numbers are measured and some are illustrative, and the artboards do not always say which. The multi-line station drafts carry real weekday boardings from the committed rail payload — Union Station at A 4,818, B 4,093 and D 2,571, averaged over Jul 2025 to Jun 2026. The panel drafts use illustrative figures in the same shape. Where the two disagree about a stop, the measured set is the true one. Judge the design, not the data.
EVERY MAP HERE IS DRAWN No artboard renders a real basemap. MapLibre will not paint in this preview, so the basemap, the route geometry and the station positions are hand-drawn stand-ins for the committed line geometry. Station spacing and label collisions are therefore suggestive rather than accurate, which matters most for the decluttering and zoom drafts.

WHAT HOLDS IN EVERY DRAFT A row is one stop on one line, never a stop's lines added together. A month with no report is drawn as a gap, never as a zero. The words are always Boardings and Alightings, and Change is boardings less alightings within one month. Every control shown is state that a shared link carries.
HOW TO COMMENT Comment on the artboard you are reacting to rather than here, and say which reading it made easy or hard. The most useful reactions name a question a draft stopped you answering. A comparison between two drafts on one page is worth more than a verdict on one, because the drafts on a page are alternatives.
