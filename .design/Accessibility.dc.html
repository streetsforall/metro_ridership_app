The badge is a glyph channel because the fill channel is spoken for Painting a station by its accessibility would take the one channel this project reserves for line identity, so the state rides a badge whose shape carries the distinction and whose ink only reinforces it. The two inks separate by ΔE 20.1 for a deuteranope, and the badge would still read with no colour at all. The three states also follow the app's existing discipline about missing data: no information is its own state and never collapses into not accessible, exactly as a month a stop did not report is a gap and never a zero.
