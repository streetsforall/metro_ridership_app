Stop Ridership · map No measure toggle — both figures are always drawn
Why this covers the most ground Both figures sit on the map at once, so Change stops being a column a reader has to trust and becomes the difference between two lengths they can see. That is the gap CONTEXT.md and the roadmap keep naming, answered by the geometry rather than by a control. Both bars are tints of one line colour, so hue still says which line. The costs are real: two bars per stop is twice the ink at ~800 stops, and a stop whose two figures nearly match reads as a symmetrical smudge at low zoom.
