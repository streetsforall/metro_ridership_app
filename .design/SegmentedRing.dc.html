BB Segmented ring — the sector says which line, the thickness says how many
One mark per station. Still one datum per line.
Stop Ridership · map Boardings · weekday average · Jul 2025 – Jun 2026
A station is drawn once, and its ring is cut into equal sectors, one for each line that calls there. Each sector grows outward on its own line's boardings, so nothing is added together and nothing is hidden behind anything else. The pin at the centre is filled where a line ends, hollow where every line runs through.

It fixes the blend without proposing a rollup Each sector belongs to exactly one line and never overlaps another, so colour goes back to meaning which line, and no reader can mistake the mark for a station total because the sectors are visibly separate quantities. The cost is that thickness reads magnitude worse than area does, and a station on four lines gives each line ninety degrees of a small mark. It also needs a letter inside each sector before it is safe: the validator puts the C Line green and the B Line red at ΔE 4.9 for a deuteranope, well under the floor of 8, so hue alone cannot tell those two sectors apart. The filled pin is the second distinction a transit diagram draws that this dataset can actually support, and it comes out of GTFS with one caveat: taking the first and last stop of every trip gives the A Line nine termini, because short turns and depot moves end wherever they end, so the rule has to be the stops that end a large share of trips rather than any trip at all.
