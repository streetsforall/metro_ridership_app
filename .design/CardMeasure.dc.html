One control reaches three places, and that is the whole variation The card’s chart is the third consumer. F paints this control and wires nothing to it. Here it sizes the circles, ranks the rail and chooses the series in the card, so a reader who wants the alightings picture gets all three views of it rather than one. R makes the same control mean less, and both readings are defensible. Draft R draws boardings and alightings on the map always, which leaves the control ordering the list and nothing else. F₂ is the opposite bet: one measure everywhere, and Both is a mode a reader asks for rather than the default. 29 bus stops report ridership with no coordinate — 16 whose name is used by two places and 13 with no GTFS match — so the map cannot show them. Every rail stop has one. The map is drawn here as a placeholder; the real one is MapLibre with the committed route geometry.
