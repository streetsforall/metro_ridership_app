THE CHANNELS Every way a mark can say how many, ranked by how well people read it Cleveland and McGill ordered these by decoding accuracy: position, then length, then angle, then area, then lightness, then texture and hue. The app encodes ridership as circle area, which is fourth. Length is the strongest channel nobody here has spent.
The circle layer has no channel left to give, so every option below is a new layer. In Map.tsx, radius carries magnitude, fill and stroke colour carry which line and the selection ring, and opacity and stroke width carry which measure through MEASURE_PAINT. Only circle-blur is unused, and blur reads as uncertainty rather than size.

RULED OUT Blur says uncertain, not large. Animation or pulse rate fails prefers-reduced-motion, and two marks cannot be compared unless a reader sees them in the same instant. Shape family is a categorical channel and carries no order, so a square never reads as more than a triangle.
THE RULE THAT CUTS ACROSS ALL OF THEM Any channel whose smallest step looks like nothing breaks the gap rule — a one-pixel spike, the palest shade, a zero-degree wedge all make “reported almost nobody” look like “did not report”. Whichever wins needs a floor on the smallest drawn value and a separate not-reported mark. Every draft on this page uses the same one: a dashed grey ring.
